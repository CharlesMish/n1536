# Review of the September 22 external audit

Starting main: `7941015830a73ad454b7760e78cbc807f62835d5` (merged presentation PR #9).

The review identifies useful presentation weaknesses, but its proposed simultaneous comparisons are design alternatives. This repair preserves the author's case-switching format. It does not change the numerical specimens to make their differences look larger.

## Confirmed corrections

- **SAMPLES:** the large escaping purple curve was the cardinal influence function, not the degree-8 fit. Direct evaluation of the shipped kernel gives the fit a sampled range of −0.422399… to 1.034991…, hence the correct displayed span 1.46. The selected cardinal reaches −2.319368…, outside the main axes. Influence now stays in its own scaled and labeled inset; the measured reconstruction remains bright on fixed axes. Transitions actually crossfade finished curves, and endpoint Span/Bend readings wait until the crossfade ends. The chart clears the header, controls, reading, and inset on desktop; compact windows use document flow.
- **VOLUME:** reproduced the field's sink label overlapping the subtitle and repaired the layout. The alleged stale *completed* status did not reproduce: the real Carry run completed at iteration 51, its pending label became hidden, and mechanical/reference became 1.00. Thermal/reference correctly remained unavailable until Conduct completed at iteration 37. That lifecycle was poorly explained. The page now distinguishes the selected intermediate/completed result from the background solver's current obligation and iteration, labels missing references explicitly, and handles interrupted runs. Numerical workers and stopping criteria are unchanged.
- **DIVERGENCE:** replaced “every closed curve … its interior” with the divergence theorem for a region and its boundary on the periodic surface. A winding loop need not bound a region. Added the screen-down positive/clockwise convention directly to the Turn readout.
- **MAGNITUDE:** replaced the source slogan “Structure is the thing you keep” with wording that distinguishes the original phases from the magnitude held across cases.
- **FIT:** the catalog still promised all four datasets on opening after PR #9 made Dataset I the selected opening view. Corrected the catalog to match the four-choice exhibit; the full comparison remains in the study.

## Disposition of the remaining suggestions

| Study | Check / disposition |

| Law | Exact modular map, inverse, start distances, and finite-grid caveats remain valid. Step 32 is an intentional separated state; restart exposes step 0. A distance inset would be an enhancement, not a fix to the trajectories. |

| Degrees | Graph/BFS/minimum-cut checks pass. Screen positions are explicitly frozen across the three cases, so moving Apart's vertices would change the current visual contract. Crossings are not graph vertices. |
| Impulse | The suspected late pulse cannot occur within the allowed slider range: 0.2 + 2.2 + 0.6 = 3 seconds. Force support and exact impulse are already tested throughout that range. |
| Eigenvalues | Exact trajectories, stationarity, and the fixed-start peak checks pass. The small k=4 peak is real and intentionally modest. |

These dispositions do not claim that every possible layout or interaction is flawless. They distinguish reproducible defects from alternative exhibit designs and retain the existing mathematical limits.
